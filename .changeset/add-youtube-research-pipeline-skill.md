Add `recipe-youtube-research-pipeline` skill: searches YouTube for 10 relevant videos via `yt-search`, ingests them into a new NotebookLM notebook, runs AI-powered topic analysis, optionally generates a deliverable (flashcards, infographic, mindmap, audio), and saves a fully-cited markdown research report to the vault with complete source metadata (view counts, channel names, engagement ratios).
